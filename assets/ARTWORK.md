# Artwork

File: [square cover](thumbnail.png) (1254×1254, shown by Arsenal and HD2MM).

Drawn in code with Claude Opus 5.5 (Pillow and NumPy); no image generator was used. The cover is the Holster Your
Shield option's cover in Vanilla Plus Megapack: its `assets/source/covers.py` (`HolsterYourShield`) draws it with the
shared renderer of the Bingus Shared Loader, Mod Bindings Menu and Megapack art, and `option_images.py` renders it.

- Franklin Gothic Heavy for the title, Bahnschrift SemiBold (width 87) for everything else, ground `#121110`, cream
  `#f0e8d6`, yellow `#fdd00c`.
- The hero is a Helldiver in side profile with a pistol held forward and the shield slung on the back by a strap; a
  grey dashed outline in front at chest height marks where the shield was held, and a yellow arrow loops over the
  head onto the back. The side text says it: "shield on your back".
- The PNG carries no metadata chunks.
