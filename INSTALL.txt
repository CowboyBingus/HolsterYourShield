Holster Your Shield v1.0
Supports Helldivers 2 Steam build 25480438 / EXE 1.8.46015.0.

Install Bingus Shared Loader v18 or newer and import this ZIP into Arsenal or
HD2MM. Enable and deploy both, then restart Helldivers 2. Keep the shared
loader as the winning Wwise startup replacement.

What it changes, with the SH-20 Ballistic Shield or the SH-51 Directional
Shield worn:
- Press Backpack Function: the shield goes onto your back while you keep your
  one-handed weapon in hand, and stays there until you press again.
- Press again: the shield returns to your hand (with a one-handed weapon out),
  or with the next one-handed weapon you take.
The key is the game's own Backpack Function action (your binding applies).
No options. Other backpacks and other players' Helldivers are never touched.

Log: %LOCALAPPDATA%/CowboyBingus/Helldivers2/Logs/HolsterYourShield.log
